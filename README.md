# mindcubby

Browser-based tools for 3D printing and model visualization.

---

## <img src="3d/apps/rotater/rotater.png" width="72"> Rotater

**View and export rotating 3D STL models as animated GIF, MP4, or PNG — entirely in your browser.**

- **No installation needed** – Upload an STL and go
- **Export formats** – GIF, MP4, or PNG
- **Client-side processing** – Your files never leave your machine
- **Customizable** – Adjust spin, tilt, wobble, and appearance

[**Try Rotater Now**](https://dreisdesign.github.io/mindcubby/3d/apps/rotater/) | [Docs](https://github.com/dreisdesign/mindcubby/blob/main/3d/apps/rotater/README.md) | [Python CLI](https://github.com/dreisdesign/mindcubby/blob/main/3d/apps/rotater/stl_rotate.py)
